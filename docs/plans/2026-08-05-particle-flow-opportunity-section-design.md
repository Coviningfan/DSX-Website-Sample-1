## Opportunity section

Retain the approved headline and missed-opportunity framing. Replace the two detached quotations with a single illustrative live-call timeline: incoming call, request understood, availability checked, and appointment confirmed. The example is explicitly labeled to avoid presenting illustrative timestamps as measured performance. The finished state emphasizes the completed business action rather than the AI itself.

## Psychology

The timeline applies the availability heuristic by making the outcome easy to picture, the goal-gradient effect by showing visible progress toward completion, and uncertainty reduction by showing what the system does after answering. It avoids unsupported statistics, urgency claims, and invented customer evidence.
